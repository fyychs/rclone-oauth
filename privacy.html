Privacy policy

This application is a private installation of rclone and is used only by its owner.

It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to store and read the owner's encrypted backups. It does not collect, store, share or sell data of any other person, and Google user data is used for no other purpose than these backups.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
